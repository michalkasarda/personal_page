## About (≤2600 chars)

```
PhD student in the Vision for Robotics and Autonomous Systems (VRAS) group at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Czech Technical University in Prague.

My research develops deep learning surrogates for computational fluid dynamics with two focus areas:
• Real-time volumetric wind prediction around terrain, enabling safer UAV planning close to the ground. Delivered inside the Kolibriq project in collaboration with Lockheed Martin.
• Data-driven sloshing surrogate models replacing CFD in Monte-Carlo AOCS validation for spacecraft — the SLAAP activity under ESA FIRST! / FLPP, together with OHB System and OHB Hellas.

Before starting my PhD I worked on visual teach-and-repeat navigation at the Intelligent Mobile Robotics group at CIIRC.

Interests: deep (reinforcement) learning, computational fluid dynamics, UAV planning & control, SLAM.
```
